# Development Guidelines & Rules

## Container & Card Styling Rules
Whenever a new option, card, module, or list item is added to any screen (especially in Home, Dashboard, or System screens), strictly ensure that its container styling matches the standard design system:
1. **Borders & Outlines:** The border stroke width, outline color, and shape must be identical to the standard cards (such as the "DNS Changer" or "IP Address Lookup" cards).
2. **Consistency:** Do not use default or mismatched border styles for newly created UI components. Every container card across the entire app must remain completely uniform, clean, and symmetrical.

## Generator Action Buttons
- Keep action buttons clean and minimal with concise, bold text labels (e.g., "Generate Username", "Generate Name", "Mix Names", "Find Names").
- Do not include leading sparkle/star icons or emojis inside the primary action buttons.
